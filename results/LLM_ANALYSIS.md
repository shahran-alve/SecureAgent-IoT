**Method.** Pairing unit (model, condition, system, seed, attack_id, scenario_id); every styled attempt is paired with its own no-injection control. Estimates are mean paired differences in percentage points (utility: in utility units). Intervals: percentile 95% cluster bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, cluster = (attack_id, scenario_id) for the attack benchmark and scenario_id for the response benchmark. p: two-sided cluster-level sign-flip permutation test, 10,000 permutations; p_holm: Holm-adjusted within the numbered family (main models and Phi-4-mini are separate families). Fixed seeds; rerunning gives the same numbers. Full tables: `results/tables/LLM_analysis_*.csv`. Testbed-synthetic scenarios; not field measurements.

**Floor on p-values.** With 10,000 permutations the smallest attainable p is 1/10,001 = 0.00010. Holm multiplies the smallest p by the family size, so the smallest attainable adjusted p is about 0.030 in family 1 (300 tests), 0.010 in family 1_phi (100 tests), 0.008 in family 2 (84 tests), 0.003 in family 2_phi (28 tests), 0.005 in family 3 (48 tests), 0.002 in family 3_phi (16 tests). An adjusted p at that floor means the effect is as significant as this test can show.
